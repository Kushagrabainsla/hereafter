    How to play

    Controls

    Fight through the Night Market, a back-alley market in San Jose at dusk.

    Move
    ↑←↓→ or WASD
    Look around
    Click and drag to move the view
    Run
    Hold Shift
    Jump
    Space
    Crouch
    Hold C or Ctrl
    Slide
    Crouch while running
    Mantle
    Space facing cover or a ledge
    Lean
    Hold Q or E
    Grenade
    G throws where you look
    Smoke grenade
    T throws a smoke screen
    Attack
    Click or press F
    Knife stab
    Right-click or V; fatal from behind
    Aim
    Hold right-click with a gun: steadier, slower
    Pick up
    E near a gun, magazines or a drop
    Open and close
    E at a door or the food truck's hatch
    Kick a door open
    Attack a shut door with the knife
    Weapons
    1 knife · 2 pistol · 3 rifle
    Reload
    R swaps in a spare magazine
    Respawn
    Enter after elimination
    Scoreboard
    Hold Tab
    Sound
    M mutes or unmutes
    FPS mouse look
    Double-click the world; Esc releases it
